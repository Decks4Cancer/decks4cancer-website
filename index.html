Why We Exist

This One's Personal.

Decks 4 Cancer was born from seeing first-hand what cancer can do to a family.

My mum, Dorothy, has spent a decade fighting breast cancer. What began with a small tumour eventually became a fight for her life. In late 2022, our family received the call that Mum may have only three weeks to live and wasn't expected to see Christmas.

She proved that timeline wrong.

Her journey has had more ups and downs than I could ever fit on this page. There were too many hospital visits, incredibly difficult periods and times when we genuinely didn't know what was going to happen next. But she kept fighting.

In 2026, Mum was finally given the news we'd spent years hoping for — she was cancer-free. She still takes chemotherapy tablets and undergoes regular checks, but after everything she'd been through, hearing those words meant more than I can put into words.

Cancer Wasn't Done With Our Family.

Just a few days before Christmas in 2022, my nine-year-old nephew Asher suffered a seizure. What followed was the discovery of a large tumour near his pituitary gland, along with many more tumours around his brain and down his spine.

Asher went through treatment and fought incredibly hard, but on 10 March 2024, we lost him. He was just 10 years old.

Asher's fight, the impact his loss had on our family, and the countless lives he touched are things that will stay with us forever.

Why Decks 4 Cancer

Mum's decade-long fight and Asher's journey showed me cancer from two very different sides.

They're the reason Decks 4 Cancer exists.

The idea is to bring together artists, musicians, athletes and communities to create one-of-a-kind skateboard decks. A deck might begin as a blank canvas in the hands of an artist and later become something even more unique through signatures, collaborations and the people who become part of its story.

Those decks can then be used to raise money with the goal of helping people facing their own cancer journey.

We're only at the beginning.

But if we can take art, skate culture and the support of some incredible people and turn it into something that helps someone going through one of the hardest periods of their life, then we're doing what Decks 4 Cancer was created to do.

We Want To Turn Something Devastating Into Something That Can Do Some Good.

The Decks

Every Deck Has A Story.

It Starts With A Blank Deck.

The blank decks are donated by Folklore Skateboards, engraved with the Decks 4 Cancer logo and artist/s name, and sent out to artists who turn them into one-of-a-kind pieces of art.

From there, a deck's story might continue through another artist, a musician, an athlete, a band, or someone else willing to put their name on it and make it something that can't be replicated — a genuine collectable with its own story.

Some decks may be created by a single artist. Others may pass through multiple hands before they're finished. No two will be the same.

Our First Decks Are Coming Soon.

As they're completed, you'll be able to explore each deck here, meet the people behind it, follow its story and see how you can get involved.

Help Us Deck Cancer In The Face.
